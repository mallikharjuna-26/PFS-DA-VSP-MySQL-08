**⭐ A NOTE TO STUDENTS**

Learning SQL becomes easier when you practise consistently. Start with simple queries, understand the output, and gradually move towards complex joins, subqueries, and real-world database scenarios.

Do not just memorise queries. Understand the logic behind them, practise independently, and learn to explain your approach clearly.

Your progress is reflected in the problems you can solve and the queries you can explain.
