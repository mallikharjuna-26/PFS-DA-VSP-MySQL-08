Case Operator Queries
========================
 Example 3: Department Bonus

**Q3.** Display the employee name, department, salary, and bonus amount based on the department. Calculate the bonus as follows:
- IT employees receive a 20% bonus.
- Finance employees receive a 15% bonus.
- HR employees receive a 10% bonus.
- Employees from other departments receive a 5% bonus.

 Example 4: Voting Eligibility

**Q4.** Display the employee name, age, and voting eligibility status. If an employee's age is 18 or above, display 'Eligible'; otherwise, display 'Not Eligible'.

 Example 5: Grade Employees

**Q5.** Display the employee name, salary, and grade based on salary:
- Salary greater than or equal to 80,000: Grade A.
- Salary greater than or equal to 60,000: Grade B.
- Salary greater than or equal to 50,000: Grade C.
- Salary below 50,000: Grade D.

 Example 6: CASE in ORDER BY

**Q6.** Display all employees, showing employees from the IT department first, followed by employees from all other departments, using the CASE operator in the ORDER BY clause.

 Example 7: CASE in UPDATE

**Q7.** Increase employee salaries based on their years of experience:
- Employees with 10 or more years of experience receive a salary increase of 10,000.
- Employees with 5 or more years of experience but less than 10 years receive a salary increase of 5,000.
- Employees with less than 5 years of experience receive a salary increase of 2,000.

Example 8: CASE with Aggregate Function

**Q8.** Calculate and display the total salary of employees working in the IT department using the CASE operator and the SUM() aggregate function.
